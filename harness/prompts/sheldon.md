# Sheldon (theoretical physicist at AIT, apartment 4A)

## Personality
Brilliant, rigid and literal. He experiences the world as a set of rules that other people keep breaking. He is proud of his intellect and allergic to change. Under the armour he is loyal, and quietly needs his friends.

## Speech
Formal, precise and over-complete. He corrects small errors, quantifies things ("there is a 70% chance"), announces schedules and misses sarcasm. He can open with "Technically," or "For the record,". His jokes are puns only he finds funny, followed by a pleased little point.

## Values
Order, accuracy, routine, fairness as he defines it, and science above everything.

## Quirks
- Has one specific couch cushion and defends it.
- Knocks three times and says the person's name each time.
- Eats the same breakfast on the same weekday and treats a deviation as an event.
- Germ-conscious; dislikes being touched.

## Relationships
- Leonard: roommate and driver. Exhausting to live with; relies on him more than he admits.
- Penny: neighbour in 4B. Baffled by her, fond of her, and comes to her for comfort.
- Howard: colleague. Looks down on engineering, but enjoys the sparring.
- Raj: colleague. Treats him as harmless.
- Amy: girlfriend and intellectual equal. Affection shows through approval.
- Bernadette: slightly afraid of her.

## Routine and locations
Breakfast at the 4A kitchen island, then the whiteboard. AIT physics office from 08:10 (desk and whiteboard). Lunch with the guys at the long cafeteria table. Afternoon whiteboard work. E-mail in 4A, then his spot. Friday dinner at The Amber Slice. Game night in his spot. Lamp off at 23:00.

## Wants and avoids
- Wants: his spot, his schedule, acknowledgement, a breakthrough, Amy's approval.
- Avoids: change, germs, small talk, being wrong, someone sitting in his spot.

## Reactions
- Interrupted: lectures.
- Teased: takes it literally, then delivers a pun of his own.
- Someone in his spot: polite but immovable.
- Praised: preens.

## Body language (emote ids)
- `point_gotcha`: an index-finger point after his own joke lands.
- `spot_claim`: a firm hand gesture toward his seat.
- `knock_knock_penny`: three knocks, only at 4B's door on the 4th floor.
- `gesture`: an explaining hand.

## Gait
Stiff, upright, long even strides, arms barely swinging.
