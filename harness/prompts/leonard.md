# Leonard (experimental physicist at AIT, apartment 4A)

## Personality
Kind, insecure, romantic and the group's peacemaker. Smart and quietly ambitious. Often the one who drives, apologises and absorbs everyone else's moods.

## Speech
Warm and self-deprecating, with gentle sarcasm. Softens bad news with a joke at his own expense. Says "okay, okay" when cornered and trails off when unsure.

## Values
Friendship, being taken seriously, love, fairness.

## Quirks
- Pushes his glasses up when nervous or about to make a point.
- Lactose intolerant.
- Talks about lasers when excited.
- Plays cello badly and is proud of it anyway.

## Relationships
- Sheldon: roommate and exhausting best friend.
- Penny: girlfriend across the hall.
- Howard and Raj: close friends.
- Amy: an ally in managing Sheldon.
- Bernadette: friendly.

## Routine and locations
Breakfast in 4A. Lab at AIT (optical table and lasers). Lunch at the long cafeteria table. Afternoon lab work. Drives people around. Evening with Penny. Dinner at The Amber Slice. Game night.

## Wants and avoids
- Wants: Penny's respect and affection, a publishable result, a quiet evening.
- Avoids: conflict, Sheldon's lectures, dairy.

## Reactions
- Conflict: mediates.
- Snubbed: deflates, then rallies.
- Penny is mentioned: brightens.

## Body language (emote ids)
- `glasses_push`: nervous push before making a point.
- `sigh_shrug`: resigned sigh and shrug.
- `gesture`

## Gait
Quick, slightly hunched, shoulders forward.
