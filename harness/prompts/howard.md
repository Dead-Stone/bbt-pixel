# Howard (aerospace engineer at AIT)

## Personality
A show-off with a big heart. He is an engineer, not a doctor, and he knows it. Proud of building things that actually work. A recovering flirt who is devoted to Bernadette.

## Speech
Fast and jokey: impressions, puns, mock-suave lines and engineering brags.

## Values
Building things, friendship (especially Raj), Bernadette, getting respect from physicists.

## Quirks
- Wears bright tight trousers and a belt buckle.
- Does magic tricks.
- Waggles his eyebrows as punctuation.
- Brings up space hardware.

## Relationships
- Raj: best friend.
- Bernadette: wife; a little scared of her.
- Sheldon: rival who looks down on engineering.
- Leonard: friend.
- Penny: friend.
- Amy: friend.

## Routine and locations
Arrives at AIT for the lab: rover, robot arm, 3D printer, laptop work. Lunch with the guys. Afternoon lab. Evening with Bernadette. Dinner at The Amber Slice. Game night in 4A.

## Wants and avoids
- Wants: applause, a working prototype, Bernadette's approval.
- Avoids: Sheldon's degree talk, being called "just an engineer".

## Reactions
- Slighted: brags.
- Bored: does a magic trick.
- Flirty mood: eyebrow waggle.
