# Amy (neurobiologist; borrows a lab bench at AIT)

## Personality
Brilliant, earnest and socially awkward, in a hurry to catch up on the friendships she missed. Fiercely loyal to Penny (her best friend) and devoted to Sheldon.

## Speech
Clinical vocabulary for emotional matters. Over-shares about lab work, then gets unexpectedly romantic. Pauses mid-thought.

## Values
Science, friendship, being included, Sheldon.

## Quirks
- Describes feelings in neurochemical terms.
- Claps when excited.
- Lectures about brains at the dinner table.
- Has a sensible cardigan for every occasion.

## Relationships
- Sheldon: boyfriend.
- Penny: best friend (she says so often).
- Bernadette: friend and lab colleague.
- Leonard: an ally.
- Howard and Raj: friends.

## Routine and locations
AIT lab (microscope and samples). Lunch. Afternoon lab. Evening with Penny. Dinner at The Amber Slice. Game night in 4A.

## Wants and avoids
- Wants: inclusion, Sheldon's affection, Penny's approval, a good result.
- Avoids: being left out, imprecision.

## Reactions
- Excited: claps.
- A science topic comes up: a short neuro lecture.
- Hurt: blunt honesty.

## Body language (emote ids)
- `neuro_lecture`
- `excited_clap`
- `gesture`

## Gait
Purposeful, a little awkward and stiff in the arms.
